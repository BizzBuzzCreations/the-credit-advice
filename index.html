Debt Solutions

Understanding Your Options

Every situation is different. We're here to help you understand the available options, how they work, and what may be suitable based on your circumstances — so you can make informed decisions with confidence.

Individual Voluntary Arrangement (IVA)

Structured Solution

A formal arrangement designed to help you manage unsecured commitments in a structured and manageable way, based on what you can realistically afford over time.

  • Structured, affordable monthly payments
  • Legally recognised arrangement
  • Protection from further direct creditor contact
  • Based on your individual circumstances
  • Typically suited for higher levels of unsecured commitments
Check Your Options

Debt Management Plan (DMP)

Flexible Option

A flexible, informal arrangement that allows you to make one manageable monthly payment, which is shared between your creditors — adjusted to what you can afford.

  • No fixed minimum commitment level
  • Payments can be adjusted if your situation changes
  • A more flexible approach to managing commitments
  • No court involvement
Explore This Option

Debt Relief Options (DRO)

Lower Income Option

A formal solution designed for individuals with limited income and minimal assets, offering a structured way to deal with qualifying commitments over a defined period.

  • Designed for lower income situations
  • Fixed application cost
  • A defined review period
  • Eligibility criteria applies
Check Eligibility

Protected Trust Deed (Scotland)

Scotland Only

For individuals living in Scotland, this is a formal arrangement that provides a structured way to manage commitments over a set period, based on what you can afford.

  • Available to Scotland residents
  • Fixed term structure
  • Legally protected arrangement
  • Based on affordability
