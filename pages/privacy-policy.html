1

Introduction

At The Credit Advice, we are committed to protecting your privacy and handling your personal data responsibly. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information in accordance with UK data protection laws.

Governing Legislation: This policy is prepared in accordance with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018. By using our website or services, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this policy.

2

Who We Are

The Credit Advice is an online platform designed to help individuals better understand their financial situation and explore the options available to them. We aim to provide clear, transparent, and responsible information to empower people facing financial difficulty.

Our Commitment: We take your privacy seriously and will never misuse the information you share with us. Transparency and trust are at the core of everything we do.

3

Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information when you interact with our website or contact us:

  • Personal details — such as your name, email address, and phone number
  • Enquiry information — details you provide through our contact or enquiry forms
  • Technical data — including IP address, browser type, and device information
  • Website usage data — pages visited, time on site, and interaction data used to improve user experience

We only collect information that is necessary and relevant to the service we provide. We do not collect sensitive personal data unless you voluntarily provide it.

4

How We Use Your Data

The information we collect is used solely to provide and improve our services. Specifically, we use your data to:

  • Respond to your enquiries promptly and accurately
  • Provide relevant and helpful financial information tailored to your situation
  • Improve website performance, usability, and content
  • Maintain internal records and ensure regulatory compliance

Marketing: We will only contact you for marketing purposes if you have explicitly given us permission to do so. You can withdraw this consent at any time by contacting us directly.

5

Legal Basis for Processing

We process your personal data on the following legal grounds as defined under the UK GDPR:

  • Consent — when you voluntarily provide your details through our forms or sign up for communications
  • Legitimate interests — in improving our services, maintaining security, and operating our platform effectively
  • Legal obligations — where we are required to process or retain data in order to comply with applicable law or regulation
6

Sharing Your Data

We do not sell or rent your personal information to any third parties under any circumstances.

Your data may be shared with trusted third parties only where strictly necessary — for example, to support your enquiry or to fulfil a legal or regulatory requirement. Any third parties with whom we share data are required to handle it in accordance with applicable data protection laws.

7

Data Retention

We retain your personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purpose for which it was collected, including any legal, regulatory, or compliance obligations.

Once your data is no longer required, it will be securely deleted or anonymised in line with our internal data retention procedures.

8

Your Rights

Under the UK GDPR, you have the following rights in relation to your personal data:

  • Right of Access — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Right to Rectification — request correction of any inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Right to Erasure — request deletion of your data where applicable
  • Right to Object or Restrict — object to or limit how we process your data
  • Right to Data Portability — request your data in a structured, machine-readable format

To exercise any of the above rights, please contact us using the details in Section 12. We will respond to all valid requests within one calendar month.

9

Data Security

We take appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data from unauthorised access, misuse, disclosure, or loss.

Our security practices include encrypted data transmission, access controls, and regular review of our internal processes. While we take all reasonable steps to protect your information, no online transmission is completely risk-free.

10

International Transfers

Where necessary, your data may be processed outside the United Kingdom. In such cases, we ensure that appropriate safeguards are in place — such as standard contractual clauses or adequacy decisions — to protect your information in line with UK GDPR requirements.

11

Cookies

Our website uses cookies to improve functionality and analyse user behaviour. Cookies help us understand how visitors use our site so we can make it better for everyone.

For full details on the cookies we use, how long they last, and how to manage your preferences, please refer to our Cookies Policy.
